# Arrangement sweep — arr-a

Successive halving, eta = 2, 5 rounds, common random numbers inside every round
(docs/SIM-PLAN.md §8). Depth 3. Round 1 starts with 400 random back ranks and
20 games each; every round doubles the games and keeps the better half.

The ranking key is **imbalance = |white score − 0.5|**, ties broken by the interest score. Balance and interest
stay two axes; the table prints both, and the gate column prints the rejects.

## Exploratory shortlist — rejection gates NOT enforced

The gate column prints rejects; this tool still keeps the better half regardless of them. That is
deliberate while no sweep is needed, and it is why these are not "survivors": an approved setup
filter must enforce the gates before it selects anything (docs/TAKEOVER-PLAN.md §2). Treat the
shortlist as a hypothesis for a future, gate-enforcing sweep, not as accepted arrangements.

Round 1 numbers are noisy on purpose: at 20 games one arrangement's score carries about
±0.22. Halving spends the budget on the survivors, so trust the last round only,
and confirm it at a second depth before acting.
