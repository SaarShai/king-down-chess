Three image sheets cut and packed into one atlas (six puffs, three gusts), on the owner's plan image tool with the Stratus cloud bank and king as guides.

Puffs:
Sprite sheet for a fantasy chess board game. Exactly six separate painted cloud puffs in a grid of 3 columns and 2 rows on a completely flat, pure chroma green background (#00FF00), with wide empty green space between them; none touches another or the image edge. Match the guide images: the same hand-painted gouache as the painted cloud bank and the painted king figure, with warm ivory highlights on the tops of the lobes, pale blue-gray shade in the folds and underbelly, and a broken, thin, darker slate-blue painted line along the lower edges of the lobes, like the inked edge of the figure. Each puff is one chunky fused group of four to seven irregular rounded lobes, wider than tall (about 3:2), with a flatter underside and a dense, fully opaque middle. Each puff has a different silhouette, all with the same light from the upper left and the same scale. Crisp painted edges on the outline, no blur, no glow, no thin wisps, no separate small fragments, no holes. Not vector, not glossy, not spheres, not photographic. No ground, no shadow on the background, no text, no figure.

Gusts:
Sprite sheet for a fantasy chess board game. Exactly three separate painted wind gusts stacked in 3 rows on a completely flat, pure chroma green background (#00FF00), with wide empty green space between them; none touches another or the image edge. Match the guide images: the same hand-painted gouache as the painted cloud bank and the painted king figure, in warm ivory with pale blue-gray shade and a broken thin darker slate-blue painted line on the lower edge. Each gust is one long horizontal ribbon of cloud-like air about five times wider than tall, flowing left to right: the right end is a thick rounded head that curls over itself in one soft scroll like a stylised storybook wind, and the body thins smoothly toward the left into a single fine pointed tail. Each gust has a different curl, all the same scale. Solid opaque paint with crisp painted edges, no blur, no glow, no spray, no separate fragments. Not vector, not photographic. No ground, no shadow on the background, no text, no figure.
