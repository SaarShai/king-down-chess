## Notes for the owner and the lawyer

These notes are not part of the published text. The published text starts at "Contact King Down". The last part, "How the owner handles the mailbox", is an internal procedure.

- **Purpose.** The page gives players and authorities an address that a person reads. Ofcom can send an information notice. A provider that does not answer it by the deadline can get a fine. Ofcom fined 4chan £20,000, plus £100 a day, for such a failure.
- **One address or several.** The live pages use `hello@kingdown.dev`. The drafts name a safety address, an appeals address and a complaints address. They can be one mailbox with different subject lines. [owner fact: the choice]
- **Affected persons who are not players.** The planned report routes need a sign-in. This page is the route for a person who is not a player, for example a person in an intimate image report. See gap 1 in the [record of measures](uk-record-of-measures.md).
- **EU.** If a lawyer finds that the Digital Services Act applies, the same page serves Article 11 and Article 12. Article 11 asks for a contact point for authorities. Article 12 asks for a contact point for users, and it must not rely only on automated tools. A provider with no EU base also needs a legal representative (Article 13). [owner fact: lawyer's answer]
- Items in square brackets are open. Each one is in the list "Facts only the owner can supply".

## How the owner handles the mailbox (internal)

1. **Read it every working day.** [owner fact: who reads it, and when they are away]
2. **Note the time of receipt.** The 48-hour limit for an intimate image report starts at receipt.
3. **Sort each message** by subject: report, appeal, complaint, data request, authority, other.
4. **Child sexual abuse content, or a threat to a person's life:** act at once. Do not forward the content. Follow the escalation path in the [record of measures](uk-record-of-measures.md) (M13): report to the National Crime Agency and, for a threat to life, to the police. Set a report hold so that no job deletes the data (M12).
5. **A notice from Ofcom or another authority:** note the deadline on the day it arrives. Reply before it. Keep a copy of the notice and the reply.
6. **Keep a log:** date, type, action, time to act. The monthly counts feed the risk assessments, as Ofcom's "user complaints" input.
7. **Answer each message.** Say what you did, or say when you plan to act.
