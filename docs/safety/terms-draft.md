# Terms of service (draft for phase 1)

Version 0.1, 2026-10-10. Ticket: [05 — Safety documents: drafts](../specs/accounts-multiplayer/issues/05-safety-drafts.md). Report: [part 01, section 5.7](../research/multiplayer-2026-10-10/01-user-journey.md), [part 03, sections 8.3 and 8.10](../research/multiplayer-2026-10-10/03-technical.md).

## Notes for the owner and the lawyer

These notes are not part of the published text. The published text starts at "King Down terms of service".

- **Replaces** the live terms of 3 October 2026 (`public/terms.html`). The [table at the end](#changes-from-the-live-terms) lists the changes.
- **Reading age.** The Ofcom measure ICU G3 asks for terms written for the youngest permitted user. The minimum age is 13, so the text uses short sentences and common words. Test it with a readability tool before you publish. [owner fact: the result]
- **Access.** Publish it as plain HTML with real headings. No PDF and no text in pictures. Test it with a keyboard and a screen reader (ICU G3).
- **What the Act needs in the terms.** The ban on pornography and on content that encourages suicide, self-harm or eating disorders must be clear and must apply to every user. Then the service needs no age checks (section 12). The terms must also cover the intimate image duty (section 10(5)), the complaints route and the removal of banned groups (ICU G1, H1).
- **No age question.** Do not add a birth date or an age field. A self-declared age can bring Ofcom's child safety defaults (ICU F1, F2). Do not write "18+" to avoid the children's duties.
- **EU.** If a lawyer finds that the Digital Services Act applies, Article 14 asks for plain terms about content limits, moderation, human review and complaints. This draft already has these parts. It does not name an EU legal representative. [owner fact: lawyer's answer]
- Items in square brackets are open. Each one is in the list "Facts only the owner can supply".

## King Down terms of service

Last updated: [date]

### 1. About these terms

King Down is a chess game. You can play it at kingdown.dev and in ChatGPT. [owner fact: name of the person or company that runs King Down, and its place of business] runs King Down. In these terms, "we" means that person or company.

When you play, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not play.

Read our privacy page too. It says what data we keep.

### 2. Who can play

You must be 13 or older. We do not ask how old you are.

If you are under 18 and something here is not clear to you, talk to a parent or a trusted adult.

You can have one account. Sign in only with your own account. Do not share it with other people.

### 3. Your handle and your icon

You pick a handle. Other players see it. They do not see your real name, your email address or your photo.

Do not use a handle that:

- is rude, hateful or threatening;
- copies another person;
- uses the name King Down, a staff name, or a chess title that you do not hold;
- tells other players how to contact you, for example the name of another app;
- shows your age, your school or where you live.

We can change, hide or remove a handle at any time. Nobody gets an old handle again. You can change your own handle once in 90 days.

### 4. Messages

In a game, you can send short preset messages. You pick them from a list. You cannot write your own text, and you cannot send pictures.

Do not send the same message again and again to annoy a player.

### 5. Rules for everyone

Be kind. Do not:

- harass, threaten or abuse any person;
- send or share anything hateful;
- pretend to be another person;
- use chess engines, programs or other people to play your online games for you;
- break, overload or get around the game or its security;
- make a new account to avoid a block or a ban;
- try to find a player's private details.

**Content that King Down does not allow.** These rules apply to every user, of any age. This covers your handle, your messages and anything else that you send:

- pornography and other sexual content;
- content that encourages, promotes or teaches suicide, self-harm or an eating disorder;
- anything that shows or supports the sexual abuse of children (this is a crime, and we report it);
- an intimate picture or video of a person shared without that person's consent, and a threat to share one;
- support for a group that the UK bans as a terrorist group (a "proscribed organisation"); we close the accounts of such groups;
- anything else that is against the law.

### 6. How we keep you safe

We can warn you, change your handle, hide your handle, stop you from playing for some time, or close your account. We do this when someone reports a problem or when we find one.

We read reports about children's safety and about illegal content first.

When the law asks us, we tell the police and the National Crime Agency. We do this if we find content that shows the sexual abuse of children, or a threat to a person's life.

### 7. Report a problem

In a game, press **Report** on the player card. In ChatGPT, ask for a report or open the link that King Down gives you. You pick a reason:

- Computer help
- Rating manipulation
- Leaving or stalling
- Offensive handle
- Harassment
- Child safety or illegal content
- Other

If someone shares an intimate picture or video of you without your consent, or threatens to, choose **Child safety or illegal content**. Then choose **Intimate image shared without consent**. You do not have to be a player. You can also write to [owner fact: safety address]. We remove the reported content, and copies of it, within 48 hours.

We tell you when we have dealt with your report. We do not always tell you what we did to another player. [owner fact: the time to act on a report]

### 8. If we act against you

When we act against your account or your handle, we tell you:

- what we did;
- the facts that we used;
- the rule that you broke;
- how to appeal.

You can appeal. Write to [owner fact: appeals address] within [owner fact: number] days. [owner fact: who reads an appeal] reads it and answers within [owner fact: number] days.

### 9. Your account and your games

You can delete your account in your profile. We wait 7 days before we delete it. If you sign in during these 7 days, we cancel the deletion.

After that, we delete your profile and your data. Your finished and running games stay for your opponents. They see "Deleted player" in place of your handle. If the law asks us to keep some data after a report, we keep it for as long as the law needs.

We can close your account if you break these terms.

### 10. Daily games

A Daily game has a time limit for each move. The players choose it when they start the game. A player who runs out of time loses.

We send you an email when it is your turn. We send no more until you come back to the game. You can change this in your settings.

### 11. Our name, rules and art

The King Down name, the rules, the pieces and the art belong to [owner fact: the legal owner; the live terms say Saar Shai]. You can share links, screenshots and videos of your games. Do not copy the art or the game for other products.

### 12. No promises

King Down is free. We try to keep it running, but we do not promise that it is always on or free of errors. We can change it, add to it, or stop it.

### 13. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we change the date at the top. For a big change, we tell signed-in players. If you keep playing after a change, the new terms apply.

## Changes from the live terms

| Live terms (3 October 2026) | This draft |
|---|---|
| "Sign in only with your own Google, GitHub or Facebook account" | "Sign in only with your own account". Phase 1 adds email code sign-in. The owner decides the list. |
| "Choose a display name that is not offensive" | Handle rules, a preset icon, and the right to change a handle (section 3) |
| Fair play, the art and the name, no warranty | Kept (sections 5, 11 and 12) |
| No minimum age. The live privacy page says that children under 13 need a parent's permission to sign in. | Minimum age 13. No age question (section 2). Change the privacy page to match. |
| The live delete-data page says that deletion happens at once. | A 7-day wait; a sign-in cancels it; games stay (section 9). Change the delete-data page to match. |
| No safety, reporting or complaints text | Sections 5 to 8 |
| No ban on content | A list that applies to every user (section 5) |
| No text on Daily games | Section 10 |

Two pages must change with these terms. The first is `public/privacy.html`. It needs a handle and an icon in place of the display name and photo. It needs text on messages, reports and the minimum age. It needs the deletion wait, and how long data stays after a report. The second is `public/delete-data.html`.
