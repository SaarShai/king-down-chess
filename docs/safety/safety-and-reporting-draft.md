## Safety and reporting

Last updated: [date]

King Down is a chess game for players who are 13 or older. This page says how we keep it safe, how to report a problem, and what we do next.

### How King Down is built to be safe

- You cannot write free text to other players. You can send short preset messages from a list.
- You cannot send pictures, videos or voice.
- You pick a handle and an icon. Other players do not see your real name, your email address, your photo, your age, your school or where you live.
- By default, only signed-in players can challenge you. By default, only players who follow you and whom you follow can see when you are online.
- You do not play with strangers in this version. You play friends who have your invitation.

### What you can do

- **Block a player.** A blocked player cannot challenge you or send you invitations, and you do not see their messages.
- **Change your privacy settings.** You choose who can challenge you and who sees that you are online.
- **Use quiet mode.** It turns off the social features.
- **Leave.** You can resign or leave a game at any time.
- **Keep your details private.** Do not tell another player your real name, address, school or phone number.
- **Tell a trusted adult** when something online upsets you or worries you.

### What is not allowed

The [terms](terms-draft.md) give the full list. In short, King Down does not allow:

- harassment, hate or threats;
- pornography;
- content that encourages suicide, self-harm or an eating disorder;
- content that shows or supports the sexual abuse of children;
- intimate pictures that a person shares without consent;
- support for a terrorist group that the UK bans;
- anything that breaks the law.

### Report a problem

- **In a game or on a profile:** press **Report**.
- **In ChatGPT:** ask King Down to report the player, or open the link that it gives you.
- **By email:** write to [owner fact: safety address]. You do not need to be a player.

Pick the reason that fits best:

- Computer help
- Rating manipulation
- Leaving or stalling
- Offensive handle
- Harassment
- Child safety or illegal content
- Other

**Intimate pictures or videos.** If someone shares an intimate picture or video of you without your consent, or threatens to, choose **Child safety or illegal content**. Then choose **Intimate image shared without consent**. Or write to the safety address. You do not need to be a player. We remove the reported content, and copies of it, within 48 hours.

**In danger now?** Call the emergency number in your country first. In the UK it is 999.

### What happens after you report

1. We get your report. We read reports about children's safety and illegal content first.
2. We look at the facts. We can ask you for more detail.
3. We decide what to do. [owner fact: the target time to act on a report]
4. We tell you when we have dealt with it. We do not always tell you what we did to another player.

### What we can do

We can warn a player, change a handle, hide a handle, stop a player from playing for some time, or close an account. We can remove the accounts of groups that the UK bans.

When the law asks us, we tell the police and the National Crime Agency. We do this for content that shows the sexual abuse of children, and for threats to a person's life.

### If we act against your account

We tell you what we did, the facts that we used, the rule that you broke, and how to appeal.

To appeal, write to [owner fact: appeals address] within [owner fact: number] days. [owner fact: who reads an appeal] reads it and answers within [owner fact: number] days.

### If you are not happy with how we follow these rules

Write to [owner fact: complaints address]. We answer within [owner fact: number] days.

### Fair play

Do not use chess engines or other help in online games. [owner fact: link to the fair-play page, when it exists]

### Help for you

If you feel unsafe or upset, talk to a trusted adult. [owner fact: help lines to list, for example a children's help line in each country. Check each number before you publish.]

### Use this page with a keyboard or a screen reader

This page works with a keyboard and a screen reader. If you cannot use it, write to [owner fact: safety address]. We help you.
