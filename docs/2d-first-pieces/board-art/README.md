# Board artwork

`biome-board.webp` is the King Down game board: the biome side of the printed board, from the Drive root `board_colored_4K.jpg` (rulebook orientation: earth top, fire right, sky bottom, ice left, gold capital in the centre 2×2). Made from the 1200 px research copy `docs/research/drive-assets/final-art/board-biomes-capital.jpg` by `../web-art.py`. Original King Down art (© Saar Shai).

Not used: the marble chess side (`board-front_back.ai` page 2, `board-2500x2500px.png`). Its marble comes from textures.com stock photos (`FloorsCheckerboard0037`), whose licence forbids distributing the content by itself; those files were removed from the public repository history on 2026-09-26.
