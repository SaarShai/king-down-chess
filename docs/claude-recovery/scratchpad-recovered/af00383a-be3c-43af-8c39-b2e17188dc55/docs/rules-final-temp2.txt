Advanced
1. Collect 2 Pike “unit cards”, 1 King “unit card” and 7 more “unit cards” of your choice.
2. Collect the corresponding units.
3. Place 1 Pike unit on each of the 2 middle tiles of the 2nd row of your zone.
4. Place the King unit and 5 more units, in any order, on the tiles of the 1st row of your zone. *you may place Pike units on any unoccupied tile on the 2nd row of your zone.
5. Place the other 2 pieces in your “keep”.
6. Collect 10 “spell cards” of your choice.
7. Shuffle all of the cards that you’ve collected.
8. Draw 3 cards


HOW TO PLAY


Win
- first player to reach 8VP
- 1 VP for each piece in the capital
- 2 VP for king in the capital
- 1 VP for each taken piece
- 2 VP for each taken king

Play
- you can play cards and pay toll for them and perform their benefits if you haven’t already this turn.


Tolls
- PLAY TOLL: you can play a card from your hand (IN-HAND) to the play area (IN-PLAY) by paying a PLAY TOLL of 1.
- MOVE TOLL: you can move any UNIT, whose UNIT card is IN-PLAY, to any tile along its RANGE that isn’t occupied by another UNIT (friendly or enemy), by paying a MOVE TOLL of 1.
- TAKE TOLL: you can move any UNIT, whose UNIT card is IN-PLAY, to any tile along its RANGE that is occupied by an enemy UNIT, by paying a TAKE TOLL of 1 (in addition to the MOVE TOLL of 1).
- CAPI TOLL: when any (friendly) UNIT lands on a tile in the capital on your turn, you must pay a CAPI TOLL of 1 (in addition to any other applicable tolls).


Whenever you MOVE, you must pay MOVE TOLL
Whenever you TAKE, you must pay TAKE TOLL
Whenever you land in the capital, you must pay CAPI TOLL
Whenever you play a card, you must pay PLAY TOLL
+1 MOVE = MOVE without paying ANY tolls
+1 TAKE = TAKE without paying ANY tolls
No TOLL can be less than 0.


Limits
- can’t have more than 2 of the same unit (and its “unit card”), except for Pikes
- can’t have more than 8 cards in-hand
- spells can only influence 1 unit, even if there are several units of the same type on-board
- always pay toll unless stated otherwise
- any TOLL cannot be less than 0
