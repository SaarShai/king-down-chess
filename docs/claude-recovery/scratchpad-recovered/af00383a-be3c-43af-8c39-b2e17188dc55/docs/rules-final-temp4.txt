Advanced
1. Collect 2 Pike “unit cards”, 1 King “unit card” and 7 more “unit cards” of your choice.
2. Collect the corresponding units.
3. Place 1 Pike unit on each of the 2 middle tiles of the 2nd row of your zone.
4. Place the King unit and 5 more units, in any order, on the tiles of the 1st row of your zone. *you may place Pike units on any unoccupied tile on the 2nd row of your zone.

6. Collect 10 “spell cards” of your choice.
7. Shuffle all of the cards that you’ve collected.
8. Draw 3 cards

Tolls
- PLAY TOLL: you can play a UNIT card from your hand (IN-HAND) to the play area (IN-PLAY) by paying a PLAY TOLL of 1.
- PLAY TOLL: you can play a SPELL card from your hand (IN-HAND) to the play area (IN-PLAY) by paying a PLAY TOLL of 0.
- MOVE TOLL: you can move any UNIT, whose UNIT card is IN-PLAY, to any tile along its RANGE that isn’t occupied by another UNIT (friendly or enemy), by paying a MOVE TOLL of 1.
- TAKE TOLL: you can move any UNIT, whose UNIT card is IN-PLAY, to any tile along its RANGE that is occupied by an enemy UNIT, by paying a TAKE TOLL of 2 (in addition to the MOVE TOLL of 1).
- CAPI TOLL: when any (friendly) UNIT lands on a tile in the capital on your turn, you must pay a CAPI TOLL of 1 (in addition to any other applicable tolls).


Whenever you MOVE, you must pay MOVE TOLL
Whenever you TAKE, you must pay TAKE TOLL
Whenever you land in the capital, you must pay CAPI TOLL (even if you are already in the capital, or only when entering INTO it?)
Whenever you play a UNIT card, you must pay PLAY TOLL
+1 MOVE = MOVE without paying MOVE toll (free MOVE), but must play other tolls
+1 TAKE = TAKE without paying TAKE tolls (free TAKE), but must play other tolls
No TOLL can be less than 0.
the text on SPELL cards can only be applied/executed once, unless stated “can”
