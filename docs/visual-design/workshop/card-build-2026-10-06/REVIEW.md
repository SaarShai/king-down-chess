## New figure requirement

The owner does not want a custom design to reuse artwork from an existing piece. Proposed direction: 25 distinct Workshop figures, in five broad groups: fast, strong, ranged, magic, support. These groups guide three suggested figures after rules are chosen. The player can also open the full gallery and choose freely. Artwork is separate from movement and rules.

Start with five samples, one per group. Check each sample beside the existing cast and at board size. Match the camera, painted surface, lighting, and base size. Give each sample a distinct outline and main feature. After approval, make the full set and the ivory and charcoal pairs. Colour alone must not distinguish two figure identities. A future game with several custom designs should avoid giving different designs the same figure.

No new art is generated or approved in this build. The independent review is offered to the owner after this layout pass.
