# Drive folders `King Down Tabletopia`, `STLs`, `X3G`, `page`, `prototypes`, `mood board`, `outward`, `show`, `video images`, `website` — inventory and assessment

Source: Google Drive "King Down", local read-only scratch copies. Reviewed 2026-09-13.
1161 real files, 12.9 GB (macOS `Icon` stubs and `.DS_Store` ignored).

These folders close the Drive. `King Down Tabletopia` changes what we know twice. Its `rules` folder holds
the editable source of the canonical rulebook, an earlier draft, the 2015 card-game rulebook, and a **2021
pitch deck for this exact project** — King Down pieces plus Chess960 on a normal board, the newest design
file in the Drive, which **redefines the archer and the beast** (§9). Its `models` folder holds **22
low-poly OBJ meshes**, 3–7 MB each; `3d files` has no OBJ, only 100–550 MB STL masters.

## 1. `King Down Tabletopia` — 81 files, 1.5 GB

The Tabletopia (digital tabletop) build. A complete, ready 2D and 3D asset set, plus rules art.

| Subfolder | Files | What it is |
|---|---:|---|
| `models` | 22 OBJ + 1 `untitled.blend` + 1 MTL | **Decimated meshes, one per piece**, 3–7 MB. All 10 units, all 6 kings, 3 pawn poses, 2 knight poses. `old ver/` holds 3 superseded meshes |
| `pieces` | 16 PNG + 1 TIF | Painted 3/4 renders on alpha, 373×1018 to 1088×1204. All 10 units and all 6 kings |
| `board` | 5 PNG/PSD + 6 JPG + 1 AI | `board-2500x2500px.png` = **clean marble 8×8 with A–H / 1–8 coordinates**. Four single-element skins at the same size. Two 4K four-player boards with the 2×2 CAPITAL |
| `rules` | 4 AI + 1 PDF + 1 PSD + 7 PNG | See §9. `graphics/` holds seven 1821×2550 king renders used in the rulebook |
| `tokens` | 4 PNG | Power tokens for Frost, Flame, Mud and Stratus only. Spirit and Shadow have none, because their powers are always on |
| `materials` | 11 | Box art and logos for the Tabletopia listing |

**No Tabletopia script or rules text file exists.** The table logic was set up inside Tabletopia and is
not in the Drive.

## 2. `STLs` — 32 files, 1.1 GB. `X3G` — empty

Print output only. **No new sculpt.** 18 `.x3g` (MakerBot G-code), 7 `.thing` (MakerBot projects),
6 `.stl`, 1 `.obj`. Sizes are 7–207 MB against 74–554 MB in `3d files`, so these are repaired and
decimated copies. Names carry the print settings: `bishop-65mm-50mic`, `Archer-148mm-200mic-left`,
`Guard-10cm`, `Beast-14cm`. Three files are new shapes, not new sculpts: `Bishop_thicker_cross.stl` and
`Knight_thicker_65mm.x3g` (thickened for print) and `hammer.x3g` (the paladin hammer alone). The `X3G`
folder holds no file in the local copy.

## 7. Useful now

- **The 22 OBJ meshes** in `King Down Tabletopia/models` — the only game-ready meshes in the Drive. Feed
  them to the voxeliser in place of the 500 MB STL masters.
- **`board/board-2500x2500px.png`** — a clean marble 8×8 board with rank and file labels. It fits the
  chosen bright, elegant UI direction and needs no rebuild.
- **`video images/range-tiles.png`** — the designer's own legend: a green square means "may move here if
  empty, may not capture"; a red cross means "may capture here, may not move here"; both together mean
  "may do either". That is the guard / archer / beast distinction the web game must show. Copy the legend.
- **The card names for every piece**, from `page/pieces-original.ai` and `page/pieces-new.ai`:
  Pike = pawn, Steed = knight, Cross = bishop, Rock = rook, Thorn = queen, **Bow = archer**,
  **Bash = paladin**, **Block = guard**, **Cog = maester**, Beast = beast. This answers open question 4
  in `drive-misc-folders.md`.
- **The 2017 rulebook move diagrams** (`rules/King Down Classic (rules) .pdf`, pages 2–4), one grid per
  piece. Use them to check `canCapture` against the designer's own picture.
- **Character voice for the UI**, from the 13 posters and the show synopsis. Example, Block the guard:
  "You shall not pass. Unless you knock me over. In which case you shall pass."

## 8. Useful later

- **Names and story**, from `show/synopsis-01.ai`: the land is the **Kingdom of Isohn**; the four kings are
  the dead king's sons (Frost for greed, Coal for glory, Stratus for honour, Mud for blood); the queen is
  **Princess Thorn**. Good for a title screen, an about page or army names.
- **The card mode**, if it ever ships: `rulebook-updated-01.ai` (2015) gives the full 4-player game —
  victory points, keep, pawn shop, ditch, base-line safety.
- **Pieces designed but never made**, from the stretch goals: the Prince, the Fool, the Ghost, the Lord,
  the Witch, the Blacksmith, and the spells Divinity, Sacrifice, Grace, Blessing, Flood.
- **The Shadow and Spirit army** — card faces in `website`, set renders in `outward/shadow`, meshes in
  `models`: two more armies with no new sculpt work. The four-element board and the tokens, for a 4-player
  mode. Video for a trailer or a tutorial: two teasers, 4 turntables, 38 storyboard frames.

| Date | File | Version | What it is |

| 2017-05-13 | `.../King Down Rules (no cards).ai` | 9 pages | Draft of the classic rules, plus one unfinished variant page |
| 2017-05-15 | `.../King Down Classic (rules) .ai` and `.pdf` | 7 pages | **The canonical source.** The `.ai` is the editable original |
| 2021-08-05 | `.../Chess Expansion Concept.ai` | 2 pages | **The newest design file in the Drive.** A pitch for this project |

Differences that matter:

| # | Source | Rule there | `RULES.md` now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2021 concept | Archer **moves 1 forward or 1 backward** only | 1 square in any orthogonal direction |
| 2 | 2021 concept | Archer **captures the 2 forward diagonals and the square 2 ahead** (diagram: 3 squares) | all 4 diagonals plus all 4 squares at orthogonal distance 2 (8 squares) |
| 3 | 2021 concept | Beast **moves 1 diagonally, forward or backward** | 1 square straight forward |
| 4 | 2021 concept | Beast "can **only** take an enemy piece diagonally **and forward**" (2 squares) | any of the other 7 adjacent squares |
| 5 | 2017 no-cards, p9 | "Other Variations. Players may also want to try: - paladin is 'held'" — the page stops there | no variant |
| 6 | 2015 v1.5 | Win at **8 victory points**, not by mate. Units on any base-line **cannot be taken**. 4 players | mate only, 2 players |
| 7 | 2015 v1.5, 2017 | Fire king is **King Coal**; earth king is **King Mud**, called **Terra** on the poster and **Gaya** in `3d files`; sky king is **Stratus** / **Celestial** | "Flame" and "Mud", "Stratus" |
| 8 | 2017 | The eight power token counts: Freeze 2, IceWall 2, Strike 1, Haste 1, Flight 1, Sacrifice 1, March 3, Leap 3 | same — **§6 decision 5 is confirmed** |

The 2021 archer and beast are weaker and strictly forward-facing — the same direction the first measured
runs point to (`sim-results-2026-09-13.md`: archer −85 Elo, beast −179 Elo against a knight), so the 2021
text may be the designer's own correction. Do not change §3 of `RULES.md` before he answers.

## 10. Third-party / do-not-ship

- `mood board` — 33 of its 40 files are other people's work: DeviantArt art with the handle in the file
  name, Games Workshop and Forge World miniatures, Kidrobot Dunny, Disney Infinity, Mega Man, Star Wars,
  Batman, Infinity Blade. The four `.ai` and three `.pdf` boards pin these images, so **the boards must not
  ship either**. Their written text is original and safe.
- `page/movies/Footage_board` — 9 clips of named people at a table. Get consent before any public use.
- Everything else in these folders is original King Down art.

One line worth keeping, from `mood board/kingdown-inspiration.ai` (2014-02-22): "The pieces for the armies
should have simple distinct shapes because they need to be easily recognizable on the battlefield, whereas
with the kings there can be more freedom" — the same rule as `readability-notes.md`, two years earlier.

## 13. Open questions for the designer

1. **Which archer and beast ship?** The 2021 "Chess Expansion Concept" — the newest file, and a pitch for
   this exact web game — makes both pieces forward-facing and much weaker than the 2017 rulebook that
   `RULES.md` follows. Is the 2021 text the current intent, or a simplification for the pitch?
2. **What is "paladin is 'held'"?** The 2017 draft starts an "Other Variations" page, then stops after
   that line. Please finish the sentence.
3. **King names.** The fire king is Coal in the rulebook, the posters and the campaign, but Ember in the
   3D files. The earth king is Mud, Terra and Gaya; the sky king is Stratus and Celestial. Which ships?
4. **May we use the Tabletopia OBJ meshes and the marble board directly?** Does the Dream Catcher sculpt
   agreement cover a new web build?
5. **Is the Shadow and Spirit expansion in scope?** Both kings have meshes and renders but no colour guide,
   and the `website` card folders for spirit, unit cards and spell cards did not download.
