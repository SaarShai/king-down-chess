## 2. The rulebook is older than `docs/RULES.md`, not newer

`Rulebook/rulebook-updated-01.pdf` prints "**RULES of PLAY v1.5**" and "Copyright © 2015 by Saar Shai" (p1).
Its internal date is 2015-09-29. `docs/RULES.md` follows *King Down Classic — Rules of Play* (2017).
**The 2017 document is two years newer. Where the two disagree, keep `RULES.md`.**

The v1.5 book also describes a different game: a **4-player card-driven game** with victory points, a capital,
keeps, tolls and spells. It is not chess. Its value for us is narrow but real. It is the **only printed
source** for the move and capture pattern of the five new pieces (p9 chart); it fixes the **final names and
colours** of the four kings and the ten unit types; and it fixes the **board layout** — an 8×8 grid, a 2×2
capital, four zones and two neutral diagonals.

`referencebook-updated-base-print.pdf` (11 p) repeats the same chart on p9 and gives one paragraph per card.
`referencebook-updated-SnS-print.pdf` (14 p) adds the Spirit and Shadow expansion. Both are dated 2015-09-29.
`rulebook-08-high.pdf` (2015-07) is the earlier 16-page edition; the rules are the same, the layout differs.

## 3. Rulebook vs `docs/RULES.md` — differences

Read the range chart with the legend on p10: a plain green tile means "move here if empty, cannot take";
a red X on white means "take only"; a red X on green means "move if empty **or** take".

| # | Subject | Rulebook v1.5 (page) | `RULES.md` |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Win | "The first player to reach **8 or more Victory Points** (VPs) is the winner of King Down" (p2). No check, checkmate or stalemate appears anywhere | §1 checkmate, plus three draw rules |
| 2 | Players | 4 players, one per zone (p3–4) | 2 players |
| 3 | Board | "It has **4 ZONEs**"; "The 4 center tiles … are known as the **CAPITAL** and are not part of any ZONE. The tiles running diagonaly from the corners to the CAPITAL are also not part of any ZONE" (p4) | Plain 8×8. No capital, no zones |
| 4 | Safe rank | "UNITs on any **BASE-LINE** (whether yours or another player's) **CANNOT be taken**, unless stated otherwise on a card" (p4) | Not present |
| 5 | Setup | "draw **6 cards** and arrange them in order from left to right. Place the corresponding UNITs … on your ZONE's BASE-LINE" (p7). 4 pawns on the second row. The rest wait in the KEEP. The king may swap with one base-line unit | §2 draws **7** pieces plus the king, 8 pawns, order shared by both players |
| 6 | Pawn | 1 forward, move only; takes the 2 forward diagonals (p9). **No double first step and no promotion anywhere in the book** | Double step from the start rank; promotes |
| 7 | Blockers | "a UNIT's RANGE is **obstructed** when another UNIT (friendly or enemy) is located between…"; "The exception … is the RANGE of the **Knight**" (p10) | The archer shoots **through** blockers; the paladin jumps friendly pieces |
| 8 | **Archer / BOW** | Moves 1 orthogonally. Takes the 4 diagonal neighbours and the 4 squares exactly 2 away orthogonally (p9). Card: "it can only **TAKE without moving**" (ref p1) | Same pattern, same shoot-in-place rule. **Agrees**, except for blockers (row 7) |
| 9 | **Paladin / BASH** | Moves 1 in all 8 directions, **cannot take there**. Moves or takes at exactly **2** squares in all 8 directions (p9). Card: "Immediately after the controlled UNIT takes another UNIT, this UNIT is **removed to your KEEP**. This UNIT also has the effect of the **LEAP** SPELL" (ref p1) | Range of a **queen**. Self-removal agrees. LEAP ignores **every** blocker, not only friendly ones. No "cannot capture a king" rule is printed |
| 10 | **Guard / BLOCK** | Moves or takes all 8 neighbours; moves (only) 2 squares in all 8 directions (p9). Card: "This UNIT **cannot TAKE**. However, it does have the effect of the SHIELD SPELL" (ref p1). SHIELD: "the player performing the taking must pay a TAKE TOLL of 2 more cards, **as well as a KING TOLL**" (ref p7) | Moves 1, cannot capture, **cannot be captured except by a king**. The king clause is a reading of the KING TOLL, not a printed rule |
| 11 | **Maester / COG** | **Takes only** on the 4 orthogonal neighbours. **Moves only** on the 4 diagonal neighbours and 2 squares orthogonally (p9). Card: "it has the effect of the **SWAY** SPELL", which re-targets which unit the card controls (ref p1) | Moves 1 any direction; **swaps** with a friendly piece; **long swap** with its own king on rank 1. **No swap of any kind is printed.** The nearest printed effect is the SKY LIFT spell: "can switch places with any friendly or enemy UNIT in RANGE" (ref p8) |
| 12 | **Beast** | Moves **or takes** straight ahead and straight back. **Takes only** on the other 6 neighbours. Moves (only) 2 squares orthogonally (p9). Card: RAGE, which is a toll discount (ref p1). **No chain capture is printed** | Moves 1 straight ahead, empty only; captures on **the other 7** neighbours, never straight ahead; may keep capturing. The blind spot is **reversed**, and the 2-square move is missing |
| 13 | Kings | Four kings: **FROST** (ice, blue), **COAL** (fire, red), **MUD** (earth, green), **STRATUS** (sky, purple) (p3). "the **Spirit and Shadow** Kings (from the expansion) can rule any ZONE of choice" (p4) | §4 names the fire king **Flame**; the art files name it `ember`. **COAL is the printed name.** The other five agree |
| 14 | King powers | Economy powers only: King Frost cuts PLAY TOLL by 1; King Coal adds 1 VP in the capital; King Mud copies any unit's range; King Stratus cuts one toll by 2 (ref p2) | §4 lists Freeze, Ice Wall, Strike, Haste, Flight, Sacrifice, March, Leap with per-game counts. **None is printed here**, so §4 and §6.5 must come from the 2017 document |
| 15 | Spell list | 21 spells + 1 blank: Burn, Control, Curse, Earth-Quake, Fire-Starter, Flight, Frost-Bite, Growth, Haste, Leap, Mirror, **Pawn**, Rage, Rescue, Sacrifice, Salvation, Shield, **Siege**, **Skirmish**, Sky-Lift, Strike (`Cards/AI/base/SPELLs`) | §5 misses Pawn, Siege and Skirmish. It also lists no expansion card; the packs add **Spirit-Guide** and **Shadow-Cover** |
| 16 | Piece names | PIKE = pawn, STEED = knight, CROSS = bishop, ROCK = rook, THORN = queen, **BOW = archer, BASH = paladin, BLOCK = guard, COG = maester, BEAST = beast** (p6, artbook p3–7) | §3 stops at the five standard names. **Bow, Bash, Block and Cog close that gap** |
| 17 | Spelling | The book always writes **CAPITAL**, never "capitol" (p2, p3, p4, p11) | Not applicable; earlier reviews report "CAPITOL" from the 2014 drafts |

Rows 8–12 matter most. The five new pieces already exist in `RULES.md`, but three of them (paladin, maester,
beast) carry a range that the printed chart does not support. Ask the designer which chart the 2017 book uses
before any re-balance; see §11.

## 5. Board layout and colour

The die-line (`Board/סער שי שרטוט עם מידות(1).pdf`) gives the physical board: **360 × 350 mm** outer,
a **300 × 300 mm** printed play area, two 145 mm panels with a 10 mm fold gutter, 25 and 30 mm margins.
300 mm over 8 files is **37.5 mm per square**; `board_colored_4K.jpg` renders that at 500 px per square.

Biome side, read from the root 4K file (top to bottom, left to right, as in rulebook p3):
**earth on top, fire on the right, sky at the bottom, ice on the left**. The capital is the centre 2×2.
The two corner-to-capital diagonals stay plain stone, so the light and dark chess pattern survives.

## 6. Useful now

- **The two armies.** `Characters_Final/No Shadow/` gives an ivory army and `colored pieces/dark/` gives a
  black army, the same ten poses, clean alpha, no baked shadow. This is the piece set for a 2-player build.
- **The chess board.** Page 2 of `Board/board-front_back.ai` is a finished 8×8 marble board with algebraic
  coordinates. It needs no redesign.
- **Ten unit icons.** Rulebook p6 gives one round badge per unit type, including the five new pieces. This is
  the only per-piece icon set in the Drive, and it answers the gap noted in `drive-cards.md` §6.
- **The printed range chart** (rulebook p9, reference book p9). Use it to check `canCapture` and the attacker
  set against the designer's own diagram; see §3 rows 8–12.
- **Credits and names.** Artbook p1 prints "Copyright © 2015 by Saar Shai", `doubleedgedgames.com`, the
  **DOUBLE EDGED GAMES** and **DREAM CATCHER** logos and "funded with KICKSTARTER" — newer than the 2014 line
  on the box in `drive-misc-folders.md` §4. BOW, BASH, BLOCK and COG close the name gap in `RULES.md` §3.

## 8. Credits, fonts and licences

- **One credit line, and it is clear.** Artbook p1 and rulebook p1 both print "Copyright © 2015 by Saar Shai".
  The artbook adds the two studio logos and the Kickstarter badge. No artist, sculptor or illustrator is named
  anywhere in this folder, so the open question from the three earlier reviews stays open.
- **No font licence.** Every print PDF here carries **outlined text and no embedded font**. Only
  `Mats/player-mat-diagram.pdf` embeds a font, and it is Adobe MyriadPro. The Imperator family in
  `cards/Font` is still the title face, and it still carries no vendor, designer or licence field.
- **No third-party material found**, unlike `art stuff/Characters/materials` and `concept art`. The one
  exception is `player-mat-diagram.ai`, the same file that `drive-concept-art.md` §6 flags for borrowed
  links; keep it out of a press kit. The two Hebrew PDFs in `Boxes` are printer die-lines, not artwork.

## 11. Open questions for the designer

1. **Which range chart is current?** The 2015 chart gives the paladin a 2-square king range (not a queen
   range), gives the maester no swap, and lets the beast take straight ahead but not move sideways —
   the opposite of `RULES.md` §3. Does the 2017 *King Down Classic* book print a new chart? Please send it.
2. **Does the archer shoot through blockers?** The 2015 book blocks every unit except the knight (p10), so
   the archer cannot shoot over a piece. `RULES.md` §6.2 assumes it can. Which is right?
3. **Is the fire king called Coal?** The rulebook and the cards print **COAL**; `RULES.md` says Flame and the
   art files say `ember`. The same question applies to Mud versus `gaya` and Stratus versus `Celestial`.
4. **Does the web game keep the capital and the base-line?** The printed board has a 2×2 capital, four biome
   zones and two neutral diagonals, and base-line units cannot be taken. v0.1 has none of these.
5. **Who made the art?** The 2015 credit names only Saar Shai, Double Edged Games and Dream Catcher. No
   sculptor or illustrator is named in any of the 1075 files. What credit line should ship?
