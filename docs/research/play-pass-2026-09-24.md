### 4. Undo

- **Works** if you Undo while the computer is still to move / thinking: your ply disappears, pawn returns home, move list clears.
- **Fails** after the computer has already replied: Undo does nothing; both plies stay (e.g. `1. e2-e4 b7-b6` unchanged).
- **Fails** in Human vs Human: after White moves, Undo leaves the move in place.

So under the default Human vs Computer pairing, Undo only helps if you catch it before the reply.

**Result: fail (after computer reply, and in human–human).**
