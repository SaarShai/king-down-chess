# Guard levers re-measured under current rules (2026-09-17)

Old guard conclusions are void: they were measured before the paladin and pool changes.
This report re-measures the guard's three levers with fresh controls under today's rules.

## Method

- **Fresh control.** The base arm ran with `rules {}`, so it holds today's defaults, including
  `archerShots=plusDiagFwd2` and the shipped guard (`guardImmune=true`, `guardCaptures=none`,
  `guardStep=1`, `guardCaptureLimit=0`). The three control populations agree on the deciding
  metrics, so they are the same control: white score 0.541, decisive 0.791, draw rate 0.204,
  mean plies 110.8 in all three base arms.
- **Design.** Each lever: 1,600 games per arm at depth 3, seed 71, `--sample 40`, 7 workers.
  Both arms share the same 40 arrangements and opening seeds (common random numbers). The
  difference column is the mean of (rule − base) over the 40 shared arrangements, with a 95%
  normal interval. The run itself says to read its "significant" flag as "worth a second run",
  not as a test.
- **Consequential rule.** A difference is consequential when |difference| > its interval on
  **decisive**, **draw rate** or **white score**. A depth-4 confirmation (400 games, depth 4,
  seed 72, same rules) runs only for a consequential lever. No lever qualified, so **no depth-4
  arms were run**.
- Raw tables: `sim/out/pb-ab-G-cap-pawns.experiment.md`,
  `sim/out/pb-ab-G-step2.experiment.md`, `sim/out/pb-ab-G-immune.experiment.md`.
