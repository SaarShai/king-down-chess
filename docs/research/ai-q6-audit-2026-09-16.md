# Q6 corpus audit — 2026-09-16

Phase 3 of [docs/TAKEOVER-PLAN.md](../TAKEOVER-PLAN.md): can `sim/out/nnue-g1.jsonl` (80,000 games)
train anything for the game that ships today? **No.** The corpus is excluded; a replacement is being
generated under the new identity stamp. Evidence below; tool: `tools/q6-audit.ts` (read-only).

## What was checked

| Question | Method | Answer |
|---|---|---|

It cannot be *proven* from the records: no eval fingerprint is stored. Moot — the cohort is excluded on rules alone. |

The launch record (`dd34fa5:docs/claude-recovery/scratchpad-recovered/1b13f053-b37e-481e-a963-54b0069234f9/queue3.sh`) pinned
`archerMove=any`, `beastMove=any`, `guardCaptures=none`, `guardStep=1`, `guardCaptureLimit=0`,
`promotionSet=anyNonKingNoGuard` and **omitted** `paladinKamikaze`; the then-current default was
`always`, the current one is `nonPawn`.

## Decision

The plan says: *"If a subset cannot be established confidently, exclude it; preserve it as historical
evidence. Generate replacement data only for a demonstrated gap."* Both parts are demonstrated:

1. **686-record prefix** — provenance not reconstructible from any stored spec (old two-guard pool).
   Excluded.
2. **79,314-record cohort** — a different paladin rule than the game that ships. Every position
   after a paladin pawn capture, and every search score near one, describes a game that is no longer
   legal. Excluded.

Both stay on disk with `sim/out/UNVALIDATED-Q6.md`, are hashed in `docs/takeover/baseline.json`, and
must not enter a training corpus. The replacement is `nnue-g2`, generated by
`tools/q6-chain.sh` with the full resolved rules stamped on every record; its dataset, training and
staged acceptance are guarded by `tools/q6-validate.mjs`. The chain's own accept/reject report is
written when it finishes.
