## 1. Shipped fairy / variant ideas that stayed popular

### Platforms that still ship variants (one-sentence rules)

| Variant | One-sentence rule (from the platform page) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Crazyhouse** (Lichess) | Captured pieces reverse colour into a pocket; instead of moving, you may drop one onto an empty square (no pawn drops on ranks 1/8; promoted captures demote to pawns). | https://lichess.org/variant/crazyhouse |
| **Crazyhouse** (Chess.com) | Same pocket/drop idea; you may drop to block what would otherwise be mate. | https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8614995-what-is-crazyhouse |
| **Atomic** (Lichess) | Captures explode the capturer, the victim, and every adjacent non-pawn; blowing up the enemy king wins. | https://lichess.org/variant/atomic |
| **Atomic** (Chess.com) | Same explosion idea; kings cannot capture; checkmate is easier because the king cannot capture out or sit next to a blasted piece. | https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8615237-what-is-atomic-chess |
| **Three-Check** | Standard chess, but giving the third check also wins. | https://lichess.org/variant/threeCheck · https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8583983-how-do-i-play-chess-variants |
| **King of the Hill** | Standard chess, but a legal king move onto d4/d5/e4/e5 also wins. | https://lichess.org/variant/kingOfTheHill · Chess.com variants overview above |
| **Antichess** | Captures are forced; kings lose royalty; lose all pieces (or get stalemated) to win. | https://lichess.org/variant/antichess |
| **Chess960** | Same rules as chess; back rank is a random legal shuffle (opposite-colour bishops; king between rooks for castling). | https://lichess.org/variant/chess960 · https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8583977-what-is-chess-960-or-fischer-random-chess |

Lichess keeps a variant index that leads each game with that one sentence before the rules: https://lichess.org/variant. Chess.com’s variants help page lists the same core set (Crazyhouse, KOTH, 3-Check, Chess960, Atomic, Antichess/Horde, etc.) as the ones it steers players toward: https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8583983-how-do-i-play-chess-variants.

### Fairy pieces: relocate, shoot, hop, drop

**Fairy-Stockfish** (primary engine docs for what actually has a machine-readable rule):

- Predefined piece types include **cannon** `mRcpR` (move as rook on empty rays; capture by hopping exactly one screen), **archbishop** `BN`, **chancellor** `RN`, **amazon** `QN`, hopper/grasshopper atoms `pR`/`gR`/…, and flags for **piece drops**, **blast-on-capture** (Atomic), and hybrid templates such as `3check-crazyhouse`. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fairy-stockfish/Fairy-Stockfish/master/src/variants.ini and wiki https://github.com/fairy-stockfish/Fairy-Stockfish/wiki/Variant-configuration.
- README lists the games that stay in the supported set: Xiangqi (cannon), Shogi (drops), Crazyhouse/Bughouse, Capablanca (archbishop/chancellor), Atomic, Three-check, King of the Hill, Grasshopper chess, Seirawan (gating/drop-like), etc. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fairy-stockfish/Fairy-Stockfish/master/README.md.

**Chu Shogi Lion** (chain / hop / relocate-after-capture; historically dominant Japanese variant until modern Shogi’s drops replaced it):

- One sentence: a Lion moves as a king up to twice in one turn (including after a capture), or leaps to any square in the 5×5 around it; adjacent capture-and-return (*igui*) is allowed. Extra anti-trade rules keep Lions on the board into the ending — the page says Chu without the Lion becomes “tedious and boring,” and the anti-trade rules exist so Lions are not traded off early. Source opened: https://www.chessvariants.org/rules/chu-shogi (content retrieved 2026-09-24; later reloads hit Cloudflare — treat live re-fetch as fragile).

**Xiangqi cannon** (shoot/hop capture): Fairy-Stockfish’s Betza `mRcpR` is the primary machine definition above. Full Amazonia / Piececlopedia pages on chessvariants.com returned Cloudflare challenges on this date (**[unverified]** live HTML): https://www.chessvariants.com/rules/amazonia, https://www.chessvariants.com/piececlopedia.dir/cannon.html, https://www.chessvariants.com/piececlopedia.dir/rifle.html, https://www.chessvariants.com/piececlopedia.dir/grasshopper.html, https://www.chessvariants.com/piececlopedia.dir/immobilizer.html.

### What tends to shorten games / cut draws vs what drags

Evidence that is primary and quantitative:

- **AlphaZero rule-alteration study** (Tomašev et al., 2020): under matched self-play, **Torpedo / Semi-torpedo, No-castling, and Stalemate=win** were the most *decisive*; **Pawn-back** and slowed pawn mobility were more *drawish*. Extra defensive resources and slower pawns raised draws; more forward pawn mobility and removing stalemate-as-draw cut them. Draw rate also rose with longer think time in every variant. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2009.04374 (esp. §3.2 and Figure 3).
- **Lichess / Chess.com shipped “popular” set** clusters around: (1) **drops** (Crazyhouse — initiative, mates from the pocket), (2) **alternate win conditions** (Three-Check, King of the Hill — games end before arid endings), (3) **forced / explosive tactics** (Antichess, Atomic — Chess.com explicitly notes checkmates become easier in Atomic). None of those pages ship an **immobiliser** or permanent immunity piece.
- **Chu Shogi’s own text** treats the Lion’s *presence* as what keeps the game interesting, and invents anti-trade rules so the Superpiece is *not* traded into a dull ending — the opposite of “remove the sharp piece to balance.” Source: https://www.chessvariants.org/rules/chu-shogi.
- **This repo’s own lab** (not an external source, but the local decision record): immortal reach and fiddly immunity drag games; the one-step Guard is kept only because it is slow (`docs/PIECES-PROPOSED.md`, `docs/RULES.md` §6.4 / §6.9). Templar dragged draws on measurement. Ogre push exists specifically to break Guard blockades without breaking Guard identity.

**Rough mapping to King Down verbs:** Archer ≈ rifle/shoot (capture ≠ move); Beast ≈ limited Lion-power chain; Maester ≈ relocate friend; Ogre ≈ shove/relocate neighbour; Guard ≈ immunity (draw risk). Platforms that stayed popular favour **drops, alternate mates, and sharp capture rules** — not freeze/immortality.

## 2. Browser-relevant open-source engines (learn from; do not port Stockfish)

### Stockfish — weak opponents (`Skill Level` / `UCI_Elo`)

Official FAQ: Skill Level and `UCI_Elo` weaken play by **intentionally choosing suboptimal moves**. With `UCI_LimitStrength=true`, `UCI_Elo` (default 1320, max 3190) is converted internally to a Skill Level and **overrides** Skill Level. Weakening: keep ≥4 MultiPV candidates, add a randomised score bias to worse moves; the weak pick is taken at `depth = 1 + int(Skill Level)`. Source: https://official-stockfish.github.io/docs/stockfish-wiki/Stockfish-FAQ.html. Option definitions: https://official-stockfish.github.io/docs/stockfish-wiki/UCI-Protocol-and-Stockfish-Commands.html.

**Lesson for this repo:** King Down’s `src/ai/skill.ts` already follows the same *shape* (search, then soft-pick via temperature / occasional random legal move) rather than “search shallower and hang pieces.” Do not port Stockfish; copy the *weakening policy*, not the binary.

### Maia / Leela — human-move matching

- **Paper (open):** McIlroy-Young et al., *Aligning Superhuman AI with Human Behavior: Chess as a Model System* (KDD 2020). Maia is an AlphaZero-style net **trained on human games** to predict human moves, with separate models per rating band (~1100–1900). Exact human-move match reported up to ~52–53%, vs lower rates for Stockfish/Leela baselines in the same study. PDF: https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~ashton/pubs/maia-kdd2020.pdf · arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01855 (opened; not paywalled). ACM DOI page exists but the PDF/arXiv are the free primaries: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3394486.3403219.
- **Lichess first-party blog:** nine Maias, trained on >100M Lichess games; @maia1/@maia5/@maia9 bots; notes that a Maia trained on 1100s plays stronger than a single 1100 because it averages out idiosyncratic blunders. Source: https://lichess.org/@/lichess/blog/introducing-maia-a-human-like-neural-network-chess-engine/X9PUixUA.

**Lesson:** human-likeness is a **policy target**, not an Elo slider. Useful later if King Down ever trains on human games; irrelevant to porting Lc0.

### Lozza (pure JS browser engine)

- Repo / README: UCI engine in JavaScript **without WASM**, meant to run in a **Web Worker**; ships an embedded quantised NNUE `768→(256×2)→1` SqrReLU trained on ~600M positions; build concatenates `src/` into `lozza.js`. Limitations: no `stop`, no multi-threading. UCI options exposed in the bundled UCI layer include **Hash** and **MultiPV** (no Stockfish-style Skill/Elo options found in that surface). Sources: https://github.com/op12no2/lozza · https://raw.githubusercontent.com/op12no2/lozza/master/README.md · `src/search.js` (via jsDelivr) shows **hash move**, **killer**, and **history** updates on fail-high / best moves (`addKiller`, `addHistory`).

**Lesson:** template for “strong enough in-tab opponent” is **own search + small net in a worker**, which this project already chose (`docs/research/engine-ai.md`). Move ordering to keep: TT move → captures → killers → history (King Down’s `src/ai/search.ts` header documents the same stack: TT → MVV-LVA → killers → history).

### Fairy-Stockfish — variant pieces

- Engine derived from Stockfish for regional/historical/modern variants and **INI-defined** user variants; high strength via Stockfish search; GPL-3.0. Sources: README and `variants.ini` links in §1.
- Variant configuration is **movement/rule flags**, not arbitrary “on capture do X” scripts: drops, blast-on-capture, hoppers, Betza custom pieces, etc. Wiki: https://github.com/fairy-stockfish/Fairy-Stockfish/wiki/Variant-configuration.
- This project’s earlier engine research already concluded Fairy-Stockfish cannot express Archer rifle / Maester swap / Guard immunity / Beast chains without core C++ work (`docs/research/engine-ai.md`). **Do not port it.** Use it as a **dictionary of piece atoms** (cannon, hopper, drop flags) and as a reminder that config DSLs stop at displacement captures.

## 4. What to change in this repo

### (A) Do now — no new rule, no simulation

1. **`index.html` — fix the piece-guide intro.** It still says “promotion to any piece but a king.” Shipped default is the chess set (Q, R, B, N) per `docs/RULES.md` §6.13. Match Lichess’s habit: one accurate sentence, then the table.
2. **Move / capture / check sounds** (toggle + mute). Chess.com and Lichess treat this as a board essential; `src/` has no `Audio` usage. Implementation path already researched in `docs/research/rendering.md` (ZzFX, unlock on first gesture).
3. **Optional local clocks for human–human** (simple base+increment is enough). Both major apps assume timed play; Low-Time Warning is a documented Live setting on Chess.com.
4. **Rewrite the guide’s opening like a Lichess variant page:** one sentence for the whole game (“Chess960-style mirrored back rank from a fairy pool; mate the king”), then the live pool letters including **Ogre**, and keep lab king-powers clearly marked lab-only — the current dialog leads with a wrong promotion rule and a long lab-powers paragraph.
5. **Promotion convenience for the standard set:** add an “Always queen” preference (Chess.com Live’s “Always Promote to Queen”) so local games are not interrupted by a four-button picker every time; keep the full picker whenever fairy targets are legal.

### (B) Do not do

- **Templar, Catapult, Reaver** as pool/default pieces — rejected or unconfirmed in `docs/PIECES-PROPOSED.md` / measurements cited there.
- **Strike** or **Death Touch** as shipped always-on powers — lab-only in `docs/RULES.md` §4; Death Touch is exactly the “capture without moving” verb the owner asked not to recommend as a change.
- **`guardStep=2` / `guardDoubleFirst`** — measured neutral or queued; Guard identity stays one-step wall (`docs/RULES.md` §6.9).
- **`maesterSwapAny`** — measured free, **not adopted** (`docs/RULES.md` §6.10).
- **Immobiliser / freeze-adjacent, Shieldbearer immunity, Necromancer returns, Wraith through enemies** — set aside in `docs/PIECES-PROPOSED.md` (Ultima/draw / immunity / length / degenerate paladin test).
- Any **new rule or piece that needs a simulation** before adoption (owner guideline: near-equal measured changes are not adopted; hard-to-remember rules are not adopted).

### (C) One idea worth a future measurement

**Squire (in-hand drop onto an empty home-rank square, then a one-step piece)** — already sketched in `docs/PIECES-PROPOSED.md`, fills the empty “arriving” matrix cell, and is one sentence to state; Crazyhouse/Shogi show that drops raise mates and initiative rather than fortress draws, so it is the rare *new piece* idea that targets decisiveness without a fiddly lifetime flag — but it still needs a build + A/B before any pool talk, so it is not a “do now.”
