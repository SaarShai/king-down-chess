# Card paintings

The built-in image tool makes these three paintings. The March and Vault cards and the ivory knight set the style. The public files use sRGB WebP at 800 × 800 pixels. Each file has its full square view. The card window crops the view in the same way as the current paintings.

## Prompts

### strike

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square card painting for King Down chess. Create a polished hand-painted fantasy illustration in the visual family of the shown March and Vault cards: crisp broad brush shapes, warm light, rich but restrained color, clear subject at small size. A single ivory armoured knight with a burgundy crest and a spear dashes across a stone chess field. A short warm amber trail shows sudden speed. Keep the knight large and centered, full spear readable, no extra arms, no horse. Dark charcoal figures recede softly. Strong silhouette within the center square. Earth and burgundy palette. No letters, symbols, diagram, border, text, watermark or card frame. Square image.

### haste

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square card painting for King Down chess. Match the shown March and Vault card family: painted fantasy, clear broad shapes, brush texture, luminous warm light. A single ivory stone rook tower with a burgundy pennant glides swiftly across an old stone chess field. Two curved streaks of amber dust sweep behind its solid base and show a change of direction. The rook remains one solid object, large and centered. Quiet ochre sky, distant stone battlements. Rich restrained cream, burgundy, ochre and charcoal. Readable at a small card size. No letters, symbols, diagram, border, text, watermark or card frame. Square image.

### flight

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square card painting for King Down chess. Match the shown March and Vault card family: hand-painted fantasy, confident broad brush shapes, brush texture, warm cream sunlight with muted teal sky. An ivory chess bishop with a burgundy sash floats gently on a sweeping ribbon of wind over a short row of small ivory pawns on a stone chess field. Large single bishop centered, complete base visible, no wings, no extra characters. Wind forms one clear upward arc. Quiet distant hills. Cream, burgundy and muted teal. Readable at a small card size. No letters, symbols, diagram, border, text, watermark or card frame. Square image.
