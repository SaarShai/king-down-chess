# GUI materials pass 1 (CSS only)

Status: ready-for-human

Direction: plain at rest, alive on use. Materials: carved limestone, hammered brass, gilt vellum, wax red.

- Focus and armed are two channels: armed is a 2 px brass inlay inside the card or coin; keyboard focus is the outer 2 px teal ring with a paper gap (`--focus-width` for all controls).
- End Turn: a limestone slot when there is nothing to end; the brass plate (light top edge, bronze lower edge) when a move is staged. The sheen and the 2 px press exist in `turn-controls.ts`.
- Commit actions share the brass plate with a 10 px radius (`--radius-plate`).
- Spell cards: one vellum stock, one gilt rule over the art edge. Names keep Alegreya Sans 13 px.
- Board: a stone slab edge made of shadows; no outline, no size change.
- Switches and pool boxes: a limestone trough with a brass knob or a brass tick.
- Guide: the New tag is a wax-red stamp on the piece page; the list under "New pieces" has no tag.
- King Power pips: 6 px diamonds; a spent pip is hollow.
