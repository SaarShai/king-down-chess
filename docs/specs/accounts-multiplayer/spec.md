# Accounts and multiplayer

Status: research done; waiting for the owner's decisions

## Purpose

Give King Down one player account and online play against other people, on the website (kingdown.dev) and in the ChatGPT plugin. Play can be live or asynchronous (a move a day).

## Scope

1. User accounts on both surfaces, ideally one account for both.
2. Multiplayer: live and asynchronous games, invitations, matchmaking.
3. Ratings, ranks, leaderboards, profiles and awards.
