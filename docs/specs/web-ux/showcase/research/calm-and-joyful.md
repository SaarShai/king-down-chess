# Calm, minimal and joyful games: what King Down can borrow

This file has two parts. Part 1 gives the findings from twelve games and apps, each with its source. Part 2, "For King Down", gives 15 design moves. Each move says what it borrows, what the player sees, when, and for how long.

Some sources are reviews or outside analyses, not the makers. The text says so where it matters.

## Part 1: Findings

### Monument Valley (ustwo games)

- The lead designer asked why some people do not play games. He found two barriers: difficulty and time. The team kept the game short and added a level only when it had "something new to say". [Game Developer, GDC Europe 2014 talk](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-the-surprise-mobile-game-hit-i-monument-valley-i-), [GDC Vault: "Designing Monument Valley: Less Game, More Experience"](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1021380/Designing-Monument-Valley-Less)
- Rule for the art: each screen must be good enough to print and hang on a wall. The team tests for the moment of surprise in the player's eyes, not for challenge. [Kill Screen](https://www.killscreen.com/lea-schonfelder/)
- The UI is minimal. Color and characters give indirect cues. A camera mode lets the player frame a screenshot. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_Valley_(video_game))
- Small musical fragments (for example the arpeggio of a turning cog) mark each interaction. [The Sound Architect, on Stafford Bawler's audio](https://thesoundarchitect.co.uk/?p=3241)

### Alto's Odyssey (Snowman)

- Zen Mode removes score, coins, power-ups and "game over". The player keeps only the ride. [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/article/41557-alto-s-odyssey-iphone-gaming)
- Sound effects have a musical quality, so that a simple action feels "a little magical". One touch jumps; a hold flips. No menu interrupts the play. [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/article/41557-alto-s-odyssey-iphone-gaming)

### Threes (Sirvo)

- The makers added holes, monsters, walls, arrows and many themes, then removed them. One rule ("only twins can merge") made the game. [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/feature/58813/fighting-the-monsters-the-making-of-threes/)
- The player sees the color of the next tile before it comes. The player can slide the grid part of the way to see the result, then let go to cancel. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Threes)
- There is no "game over" screen. The tiles count up into a score. Each tile has a small face and a voice; tiles greet each other and say "bored" when the player waits too long. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Threes), [TheSixthAxis review](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2014/02/20/mobile-watch-threes/)

### Wordle

- One puzzle each day, the same word for everyone. The maker says this gives scarcity and asks for only "three minutes a day". [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wordle), [Boston.com / AP interview](https://www.boston.com/news/national-news/2022/01/04/he-made-wordle-for-his-partner-now-its-an-online-hit/)
- The share grid came from players, who typed colored squares to show their path without the answer. The maker kept the grid without a link, because a link looked "trashy" and the mystery made people curious. [Boston.com / AP](https://www.boston.com/news/national-news/2022/01/04/he-made-wordle-for-his-partner-now-its-an-online-hit/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2256733)
- "People have an appetite for things that transparently don't want anything from you." No ads, no sign-in. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2256733)
- The tiles of a guess flip one at a time to show their colors. A high-contrast mode uses orange and blue for color-blind players. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wordle)

### Mini Metro (Dinosaur Polo Club)

- Bold colors and simple shapes copy a transit map. To play, you join two points. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mini_Metro_(video_game))
- Every sound comes from a player action or the network. Each line has its own note; edits change the harmony. The composer wanted audio that informs and calms at the same time. Limits stop the sound from turning into noise (for example, a cap on how many passenger sounds play in a short time). [Designing Sound interview with Disasterpeace](https://designingsound.org/2016/02/18/the-programmed-music-of-mini-metro-interview-with-rich-vreeland-disasterpeace)
- Warning comes in two steps: first the passengers at a full station jump about; then a circle fills around the station. At the end, the player can save a picture of the final map and watch a replay. [Untapped Cities](https://www.untappedcities.com/build-your-own-subway-system-with-mini-metro-game/), [podfeet review](https://www.podfeet.com/blog/2016/11/mini-metro/)

### Dots and Two Dots (Playdots)

- Each dot you join plays a beep, and each next beep is higher. Close a square and all dots of that color go. [Droid Life review](https://www.droid-life.com/2013/08/15/dots-a-game-about-connecting-hits-google-play/)
- On mute, the only feedback for a square is one vibration: one strong signal for one big event. Each game lasts 60 seconds. [Quartz strategy guide](https://qz.com/82987/the-ultimate-dots-strategy-guide), [TechCrunch review](https://techcrunch.com/2013/05/01/dots-the-most-beautiful-mobile-game-ive-ever-seen/amp/)
- The game director's talk: minimal design is "a seductive trap": easy to do, hard to do well. Small elements come alive through careful animation. [GDC Vault: "Dots That Go for Walks"](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024288/Dots-That-Go-for-Walks)

### Lara Croft GO and Hitman GO (Square Enix Montreal)

- Hitman GO is a board game of figurines on nodes. Each enemy type has its own color and a fixed pattern, so the player can plan. The team removed a feature that let the player take any target, because it broke the puzzles. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitman_Go)
- Each Hitman GO level has at least three goals. The first is always "finish". The others vary: few turns, no kills, take the briefcase. The player can try each goal on a different run. [GamesBeat review](https://gamesbeat.com/hitman-go-review/)
- Enemy figurines carry a pointer on their base that shows where they look (as the Giant Bomb wiki describes them). [Giant Bomb wiki](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Games/Hitman_Go)
- Lara Croft GO: one step per swipe; the action stops after each turn. Hidden urns off the main path hold gems and relic pieces. [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/game-reviews/lara-croft-go)
- The makers wanted sound "that wouldn't impede your capacity to think", and a level where you can see the next puzzle from the current one. [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/the-making-of-lara-croft-go/)

### Into the Breach (Subset Games)

- Each enemy shows its next attack before you act: an arrow and the tiles that it will hit. A hover shows the exact order of the turn. One screenshot holds the full state. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Into_the_Breach), [Giant Bomb user review](https://www.giantbomb.com/into-the-breach/3030-58234/user-reviews/2200-31048/)
- "We wanted to make something where every death felt like your own fault." When you fail, it must be clear how and why. [Game Developer, Road to the IGF](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-subset-games-i-into-the-breach-i-)
- "We would sacrifice cool ideas for the sake of clarity every time." (Justin Ma, quoted from Rock Paper Shotgun.) [reads.mhlakhani.com repost](https://reads.mhlakhani.com/posts/582127219_10156233399322220)
- Telegraphs made battles faster. Once per battle, the player can reset the whole turn. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Into_the_Breach)

### Duolingo

- The tree became one path, because learners were "not sure" that they used the app the right way. Done levels turn gold; a tap on a gold level replays it; practice sits inside the path. [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design/)
- New animations on milestone days raised 7-day retention of new learners by 1.7%. Slack helps: two Streak Freezes raised daily learners by 0.38%. [Duolingo blog: streaks](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)
- The streak works through loss aversion. Many users say a broken streak hurts. King Down must not copy this part (the brief bans streak guilt). [Duolingo blog: streaks](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)

### Royal Match (Dream Games)

- One big Play button leads all progress. The King's portrait watches the board and gets nervous near a loss. Collected items fly to the goal that they fill. [Funovus analysis](https://www.funovus.com/blogs/royal-match-dominates-match-3-what-can-all-designers-learn/)
- "Don't slow down": the player can move while a cascade still plays. Every tap has a haptic. [This Week in LiveOps](https://thisweekinliveops.substack.com/p/5-simple-ux-lessons-from-royal-match)
- The same source says levels are "almost winnable" to sell boosters. That is a dark pattern. King Down does not borrow it.

### Headspace

- "The design should be the first dose of calm": color, space and figures act before the user reads. No pure black or pure white. The session end is a slow scale with a small overshoot, with no confetti and no badges. Controls fade during play. (An outside design analysis, not Headspace.) [Blake Crosley guide](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/headspace)
- Onboarding lost 38% of users. The fix: screens that you can leave, short animations in place of videos, a choice of path. Week-two use rose 10%. [Raw Studio case study](https://raw.studio/blog/how-headspace-designs-for-mindfulness/)

### Haptics: the guideline and the web

- Apple's guideline: use haptics with a clear cause and effect, use them sparingly, and match each one to a visible change. Overuse makes them mean nothing. [Apple HIG, Playing haptics](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-haptics)
- Safari on iPhone has no Vibration API. A trick that clicks a hidden switch input gives a haptic, but the author warns that Safari can stop it at any time. Android Chrome supports `navigator.vibrate`. [azukiazusa.dev](https://azukiazusa.dev/en/blog/ios-safari-web-haptics), [web-haptics browser support](https://mintlify.wiki/lochie/web-haptics/advanced/browser-support)

### Seven patterns across the findings

1. **One strong moment.** Each game has one signature beat (the Wordle flip, the Dots square, the Monument Valley turn). The rest is quiet.
2. **Sound carries information.** Pitch tells you who and how much (Dots, Mini Metro). Sounds share one key, so they never clash.
3. **Small, honest celebration.** A short scale or a rising note, not confetti. Duolingo shows that a milestone animation pays off; Headspace shows that it can stay calm.
4. **Cause before effect.** The player sees the cause of each loss (Into the Breach, Mini Metro's two-step warning, Hitman GO's pointer).
5. **Preview without commitment.** Threes' part-slide and Into the Breach's hover show the result before you act.
6. **A kind "one more go".** No game-over wall (Threes, Alto Zen). A retry that teaches (Into the Breach reset). A game that wants nothing from you (Wordle).
7. **Share artifacts.** The share shows the shape of your game, not the answer (Wordle), or the result as a picture (Mini Metro map, Monument Valley camera).

## Part 2: For King Down

General rules for all 15 moves. A tap or a key skips any motion to its end frame. Each motion has a still end frame for "Animations off" and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Sound and haptics never carry a signal alone. Times stay inside the brief: most motion 120 to 400 ms, one sequence 900 ms or less.

### 1. The deal: the setup flips in

- **Borrows:** the Wordle flip (one tile at a time), the Dots rising pitch.
- **Sees:** at the start of a game with a random army, both back ranks show bare stone. The pieces flip in from file a to file h. Both ranks flip together, so the player sees the mirror rule. The pawns are already in place.
- **Timing:** each flip 160 ms, 45 ms apart: about 480 ms in all. One soft tick for each file, one step higher each time.
- **When:** New game, the daily, a rematch with a new army. Not on Continue (that plays the last move, item T3 of the motion review).
- **Why:** the random setup is the first surprise of every King Down game. Today it just appears.
- **Future:** card mode deals six cards with the same flip and the same ticks, so "a random reveal" has one look in the app. A piece that you just unlocked gets a thin gold rim for 1 s as it flips.

### 2. A daily share that tells no secrets

- **Borrows:** the Wordle grid (same puzzle for all, no spoiler, no link inside the grid).
- **Sees:** on the daily result, Share copies (or opens the phone's share sheet with) three short lines and a link line apart from them:

  ```text
  King Down · Daily 8 Oct
  ♔ Won in 31 · Club
  🏹🏹 🐾 ❄️
  https://…
  ```

- The third line counts the signature events: Archer shots, Beast chains, Ogre pushes, Maester swaps, powers used. It says what happened, not where. A "Plain words" choice writes "2 archer shots, 1 beast chain, 1 Freeze".
- **Why:** the daily is the one game that all players share, so it is the one to talk about. The glyphs make people who do not play ask "an archer shot, in chess?". Today the share is one plain sentence with the back rank and a URL.

### 3. The board poster

- **Borrows:** the Mini Metro end map, the Monument Valley camera mode, "print it and hang it".
- **Sees:** "Save picture" (under Share on the result card) makes a 4:5 image: the final position on the stone board, the last move as a faint ink trail, the fallen king, the two king emblems, the date and "Won in 31" in Cinzel. No buttons, no panels.
- **When:** after any game. Built in the browser from the board canvas in less than 1 s.
- **Future:** the Workshop shares a poster of your own piece; in online play both players get the same poster.

### 4. Cause lines for captures without contact

- **Borrows:** Into the Breach ("every death your own fault"; clarity before cool ideas).
- **Sees:** when a piece falls but no piece steps onto its square (an Archer shot, Death Touch, the second and third Beast bites), a thin ink line draws from the cause to the target in 180 ms. The target falls at contact, and the sound plays at contact. The line holds 600 ms, then stays as a faint trace until the next move.
- **Also:** an illegal tap that the player expects to work gets its cause. Tap a Guard with a piece that is not the king: the Guard's base shows a shield for 300 ms, and the status line says "A guard can be taken only by a king."
- **Why:** King Down's new pieces reach over other pieces. A loss that the player cannot explain feels unfair. This joins the check line (motion item C2).

### 5. Peek before you commit

- **Borrows:** the Threes part-slide preview, the Into the Breach hover.
- **Sees:** on a desktop, hover a legal square for 150 ms. On touch, hold a legal square for 250 ms. A see-through copy of the piece stands on the square. Each enemy piece that could take it there gets a thin crimson ring and a short cause line. For a Beast chain, each bite on the path shows its number (1, 2, 3). Let go on the square to play; slide off to cancel.
- **What stays the same:** a plain tap still moves at once, so fast players lose nothing.
- **Default:** on at Beginner and Casual and in lessons; one setting at Club and Strong. It shows the rules, never the computer's plan. Today's "Show threats" setting marks all threats all the time; the peek shows them only where you look.
- **Decision for the owner:** is the peek a hint at Club and Strong?

### 6. One key for every sound

- **Borrows:** Mini Metro (sound informs and calms; limits), Alto (musical effects), Dots (one step higher for each link).
- **Hears:** `src/render/sfx.ts` already makes seven sounds with no sound files. Tune the tonal part of each sound to one pentatonic scale, so two sounds never clash.
  - Your piece lands one step higher than the computer's. You hear whose move it was.
  - Each extra Beast bite is one step higher.
  - A bigger captured piece gives a lower, longer note.
  - Check is the only sound outside the scale: the one sound of tension.
  - Each king's power has a two-note motif (for example a high glass fifth for Frost). It plays when the power acts.
- **Limits:** two sounds at a time at most; a new sound cuts the oldest one.

### 7. Three haptics, no more

- **Borrows:** Apple's rule (cause and effect, sparing, matched to a visible change), the one Dots buzz; it rejects Royal Match's haptic on every tap.
- **Feels:** a short pulse (about 10 ms) when your capture lands; two short pulses when your king is put in check; one longer pulse (about 30 ms) at the end of the game.
- **Where:** Android Chrome through `navigator.vibrate`. Safari on iPhone has no API, and the switch trick can stop working, so the design does not depend on it. Every haptic doubles a visual and a sound. One switch in Settings.

### 8. The king's portrait shows the state

- **Borrows:** Royal Match (the King's portrait gets nervous), Threes (faces that react).
- **Sees:** in each player strip, the king figure from `public/ui/kings/` is the status light.
  - In check: it tilts 6° and back in 240 ms, then holds still with a thin crimson ring and the word "Check".
  - Power ready: a gold rim. Power spent: an ink rim and a small "used" mark.
  - The computer thinks: the ring fills (motion item E1).
  - The end: the loser's figure lies down, the winner's stays up (motion item H3).
- **Rule:** no idle loop. The figure moves only when the state changes.
- **Why:** each king has a name and a power. This gives them character with no new art.

### 9. Status marks on the base

- **Borrows:** Hitman GO (a pointer on the figure's base shows state; one color for each type).
- **Sees:** a 16 px disc at the bottom-left of the square, on the figure's base: a snowflake and the turns left for Freeze; a shield for Ice Wall; a small arc for a piece under the Holy Light or Mercy shelter; "2" for a hasted piece in the middle of its turn. Each state has its own shape, so color is never the only signal.
- **Timing:** in with a 160 ms scale; out with the thaw (motion item F3).
- **Why:** the figure stays clean, and one glance at the base gives the state.
- **Future:** card effects (Firewall, Rescue) and Workshop pieces use the same disc.

### 10. Discoveries: a seal for each first time

- **Borrows:** the Lara Croft GO urns off the path, the Duolingo milestone, the Monument Valley "moment of surprise".
- **Sees:** the first time a player sees or does a signature event, a small crimson wax seal with the emblem stamps on the board's corner (scale 1.15 to 1 in 200 ms), holds 1 s, then shrinks into the Extras button (300 ms). The button keeps a dot until the player opens it.
- **Events (about 30):** an Archer shot, a two-bite and a three-bite Beast chain, an Ogre push, an Ogre that pushes a Guard, a Maester swap, the long Maester-king swap, a Guard that blocks a capture, a Paladin that leaves after a capture, the first use of each power, a promotion, a mate by an Archer.
- **The Discoveries page** (inside Extras): each found entry plays a three-step diagram on tap (motion item G1). Each entry not yet found shows a silhouette and one riddle: "An Ogre can move the piece that only a king can take."
- **Rules:** one seal per move at most; never during your own selection; the home screen shows no "12 of 40" count. The reward is knowledge only.
- **Why:** this is the tease that the owner asks for. The player learns that more exists, in the moment that it happens.

### 11. Three goals for each lesson and each daily

- **Borrows:** Hitman GO (three goals; the first is "finish"; try each on a different run).
- **Sees:** one folded line under the board: "Goals ○ ○ ○". Goal 1: win (or finish the lesson). Goal 2 uses a feature: "Give check with an Archer." Goal 3 sets a style: "Win and keep your Guard."
- **When:** a goal that the player meets fills its circle with a 200 ms stamp and one soft tick. No pop-up. A tap on the line shows the goals.
- **Why:** the daily goals change each day, so each day gives a reason to try a feature.
- **Decision for the owner:** today only a win gives a crown (`docs/PROGRESSION.md`). Can a goal also give a crown?

### 12. One path for learning

- **Borrows:** the Duolingo path (one route; gold when done; tap to replay).
- **Sees:** the Learn screen is one vertical path on parchment. First the pieces in the unlock order (Archer and Beast, then Maester, Ogre, Guard), then "Your first game against Beginner", then the kings' powers. Later: card mode and the Workshop.
- **The next node** holds the piece figure, a little larger, with one 300 ms bounce when the screen opens. Done nodes turn gold; a tap replays them. Each section starts with "Skip ahead": three short positions that a strong player can solve to pass.
- **Rules:** no lives, no hearts, no streak.

### 13. Lesson done: the piece joins your army

- **Borrows:** the Duolingo celebration with the Headspace restraint (a slow scale, no confetti).
- **Sees:** after the last correct move, the other squares dim to 70% (200 ms). The lesson piece grows to 104% while a thin gold ring passes under it (400 ms). One two-note chime in the game's key. Text: "Archer learned." Buttons: "Next: Beast" (crimson) and "Play a game".
- **Timing:** under 900 ms; a tap skips. The Discoveries entry for the piece (move 10) fills at the same time.
- **Future:** the same moment marks a piece unlock with crowns (`docs/PROGRESSION.md`: "The piece's painted figure is revealed"). Then the text adds: "It now comes into your games."

### 14. Input never waits

- **Borrows:** Royal Match ("Don't slow down": move during a cascade).
- **Rule:** no animation blocks a player. A tap on your own piece during the computer's move ends that motion at once and selects your piece. The deal, the seals, the result sequence: each one stops at a tap and goes to its end frame.
- **Why:** the brief says that joy must never slow down a player who wants to play. This rule makes every other move safe.

### 15. Retry from the turning point

- **Borrows:** Into the Breach (a clear cause for each loss; the turn reset), Alto Zen (no wall at the end), the Threes and Wordle kind loop.
- **Sees:** after a loss against the computer, the result card shows "Play again" (crimson, a new army), then "Retry from move 18", then "Review". The retry button comes when the key moments are ready (they run after the game; until then a small line says "Finding the turning point…").
- **On tap:** the board rewinds in 400 ms to the position before the costly move. The better move shows once as a see-through ghost (motion item C1), then goes. The game goes on against the same computer. It gives no crown and does not count as a result.
- **Why:** it turns a loss into the most useful "one more go". It uses the key-moment search that the app already has (`src/moment.ts`).

## What King Down does not borrow

- **The streak and its loss aversion** (Duolingo). Use weekly goals with slack, or nothing.
- **"Almost winnable" levels that sell boosters** (Royal Match). No pressure to pay.
- **A haptic on every tap** (Royal Match). Three haptics only (move 7).
- **A countdown to the next daily** (Wordle). Show "A new daily comes at midnight" as still text, not a clock.
- **Slow 400 to 600 ms page changes** (Headspace). A game screen needs the brief's 120 to 400 ms.
- **Characters that talk or loop** (Threes, Royal Match). The kings react only when the state changes.

## Where to start in the showcase

Five moves give the most effect for the least screen: 1 (the deal), 4 (cause lines), 10 (discoveries), 15 (retry from the turning point) and 2 (the daily share). Moves 1, 4 and 15 can use the real rules engine in a demo; 2 and 10 need only static pages.
