## Question

How do people play a board game well through a chat and voice agent, and what does that mean for King Down's fairy pieces and multi-action turns?

## Answer

Report: [muse-ux-and-voice-play.md](../../../research/muse-connector-2026-11-08/muse-ux-and-voice-play.md). Resolved 2026-11-08.

- Voice chess systems match the words against the list of legal moves, never free text. One fit plays; two or more give a short numbered list; none gives a reason.
- Read the board as piece lists by side, on request. Never read 64 squares aloud.
- King Down needs the verbs shoot, swap, shove and bite, and a turn can hold more than one action.
- Few tools with rich results: each first use of a tool asks for approval, so a move result carries the computer's reply and the new position.
- A link alone fails the Muse value test; it is extra only.
