# Card move text

Status: approved by the owner request to fix confirmed F-002 on staging.

The move text must describe the action that the board shows. Curse must name the card player and the moved enemy piece with its own colour. Earth Quake must name its centre and the pieces that its canonical pushes move. Salvation must name the returned piece and its arrival square. Both short text and full text use the move and the prior position. Saved history and staged actions use the same formatter. The rules and game records do not change.
