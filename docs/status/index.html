1The game

  • Setup. Chess960-style random back rank from the pool Q L R R B B N N A A G G M M S S + K, mirrored, bishops on opposite colours. No castling, no en passant. Promotion to any non-king piece.
  • Rules v0.6 (measured this session): archer and beast step one square in any direction; the guard may capture one pawn in its lifetime and step up to two squares; paladin and maester as in the 2017 rulebook. The 2017 and 2021 sets stay selectable.
  • Look. Your pick B2 "Dungeon": painted 3D voxel sculpts from your STLs, stone tiles from your Drive, torch lighting, 53° camera, orbit/zoom/pan, coordinates; 13 other presets switchable.
  • Loop. Human or computer per side, undo, resign, game-over dialog with rematch, autosave and restore, captured pieces, piece info card, copy move list, keyboard shortcuts, mobile layout.
  • AI. Alpha-beta with transposition table, quiescence and a Texel-tuned evaluation; depth 6–7 in one second in the browser.
  • Quality. 102 tests (rules perft, every fairy mechanic, AI puzzles, self-play legality, game loop, lab); 8 full games played through the real UI; 5 game-loop bugs found and fixed at the root.

2The balance lab

Headless runner on all 16 cores (9–11 games/s at depth 3), seeded and repeatable, colour-swapped pairs, Fishtest-style adjudication, pentanomial statistics, SPRT, Elo with error bars, balance and interest reported as two axes, rule toggles, runtime piece values, odds-match value measurement, successive-halving sweeps, data mining, Texel tuning. Commands: npm run sim, npm run sim:analyze, npm run dashboard.
