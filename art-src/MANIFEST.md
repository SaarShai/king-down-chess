Curated from the designer's Google Drive folder **King Down**, folder id
`1fuVE1XBIJC5G5I-vr3vzA7ED2_xFuM5r`. Curated 2026-09-13 from a full local download
(51 GB, 3,703 files) that was reviewed in `docs/research/drive-*.md`.

rulebook and artbook print "Copyright (c) 2015 by Saar Shai" with the DOUBLE EDGED GAMES and
DREAM CATCHER logos. The sculpts carry one clue only: the Windows path
