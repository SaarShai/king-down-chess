Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

King Down is a chess game at kingdown.dev, made by Saar Shai. This page says what data the game uses, why, and how you control it. Questions: hello@kingdown.dev.

Open today: sign-in with Google or GitHub, and a cloud save, so your settings, lessons and saved game follow you to other devices. Facebook sign-in, online play, ratings and unlocks are coming; the parts of this page about them apply when they open.

Playing without an account

You can play without signing in. Then the game keeps your settings, lessons and saved games only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing about you goes to us.

When you sign in

You can sign in with Google, GitHub or Facebook. We then receive from that service only:

  • your name and profile picture,
  • your email address,
  • an account number that the service gives us for you.

We never see or store your password, and we ask for no other permissions.

What the game stores for your account

  • your settings, lesson progress and saved games, so they follow you to other devices;
  • games you play online against other people, including the moves;
  • your rating and your place on the leaderboard;
  • the pieces you have unlocked.

Other players see your display name, your profile picture, your rating and the games you play with them. They never see your email address. Your profile picture stays on the sign-in service's own servers (Google's or GitHub's), and the game shows it from there while you are signed in, so that service can see when it is shown.

Why we use it

Only to run the game: to sign you in, keep your progress, match you with other players, show ratings, and fix problems. We show no ads, we do not sell or rent your data, and we use no third-party tracking or advertising tools.

Where it is kept

Accounts and game data are stored with Supabase (servers in the United States, Ohio). The website itself is served by Vercel. Both process data for us only to provide their service. Sign-in goes through Google, GitHub or Meta under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. When you delete it, we remove your profile and your saved data within 30 days. Games you played against other people stay in their history with your name replaced by "Deleted player".

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, export or delete your data at any time. See how to delete your data, or write to hello@kingdown.dev. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

King Down is for everyone, but children under 13 should sign in only with a parent's permission. If you think a child signed in without it, write to hello@kingdown.dev and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page. For a large change, the game tells signed-in players.
