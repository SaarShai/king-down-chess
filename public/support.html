Support

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Need help with King Down on kingdown.dev or in ChatGPT? Write to hello@kingdown.dev.

What to tell us

A short message is fine. These details help us find the problem fast:

  • what you expected, and what happened;
  • the steps just before the problem;
  • the time, with your time zone;
  • your device, and whether you played on the website, the ChatGPT website or the ChatGPT app;
  • any error text on the screen, or a screenshot.

Please do not send passwords, sign-in codes or your other ChatGPT conversations. We never need them.

Common questions

How do I start? In ChatGPT, say "Play King Down". On the website, open kingdown.dev and choose a game.

The board does not load in ChatGPT. Close the board and say "Play King Down" again. If it still does not load, write to us with your device and the ChatGPT version.

A friend's code does not work. An invitation code works for a limited time, and a new invitation ends the old one. Ask your friend for a new code.

A player is rude or uses an offensive name. Write to us with the game time and the player's handle.

Report a player

Email hello@kingdown.dev with your handle, the other player's handle, the game or time, and what happened. Include an evidence reference or screenshot if you have one. The King Down owner handles reports and aims to send a first response within 7 days. Keep the support reply and report reference.

Appeal a decision

If you disagree with a moderation decision, email hello@kingdown.dev. Give your handle, the action or report reference if you have it, which decision you appeal, why you disagree, and any information we should review.

You do not need to sign in to ask for a review. If you cannot sign in, tell us in the email. The owner handles the appeal and sends the reason and appeal reply through support. We aim to send a first response within 7 days. Never send a password or sign-in code.

Your data

To see, correct, export or delete your data, read how to delete your data and the privacy policy, or write to hello@kingdown.dev.
