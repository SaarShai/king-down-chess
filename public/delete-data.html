Delete your data

Last updated: 28 October 2026

You can ask to delete your King Down account at any time.

In the game

  1. Sign in at kingdown.dev.
  2. Open Settings → Account.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm. This starts a seven-day grace period.

By email

Write to hello@kingdown.dev from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my King Down data. Tell us which service you signed in with: Google or GitHub. If you use the hidden Facebook option, tell us that instead. It is hidden unless the address includes ?facebook=1.

What happens

  • A confirmed account deletion request starts a seven-day grace period. A second request during this period keeps the first date.
  • A new sign-in cancels the request. A token refresh does not cancel it. A new request after you cancel starts a new seven-day period.
  • After the grace period, the daily job deletes your account, email address, profile, picture choice, settings, cloud saves, lesson progress, ratings, rating history, badges and unlocks. It also deletes your games against the computer.
  • The job ends your open games. A game that waits for a player or for setup ends with no result. In a game in play, your opponent wins.
  • Games you played against other people stay in their history, with your name replaced by "Deleted player".
  • Your handle stays reserved. Game records and report evidence can stay. A report hold can delay deletion until the hold ends.
  • For an email request, we reply to confirm when it is done. The game sends no email or push alert.

Removing King Down from Facebook

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove King Down there: open Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find King Down and choose Remove. Then follow the steps above to delete the data we hold.
