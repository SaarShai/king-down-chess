Delete your data

Last updated: 3 October 2026

You can delete your King Down account and all the data it holds at any time.

Accounts are coming soon. Sign-in, online play, ratings and unlocks are being built. Until they open, the game stores nothing about you on our servers.

In the game

  1. Sign in at kingdown.dev.
  2. Open Settings → Account.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm. (This option arrives with accounts; until then, use email.)

By email

Write to hello@kingdown.dev from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my King Down data. Tell us which service you signed in with (Google, GitHub or Facebook).

What happens

  • Within 30 days we delete your profile, email address, settings, saved games, lesson progress, rating and unlocks.
  • Games you played against other people stay in their history, with your name replaced by "Deleted player".
  • We reply to confirm when it is done.

Removing King Down from Facebook

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove King Down there: open Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find King Down and choose Remove. Then follow the steps above to delete the data we hold.
