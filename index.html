    Letters in the setup string: P N B R Q K + A L G M S.

    Kings' powers (lab — off by default). ?kings=mud:march gives both sides a power; ?kings=mud:march,shadow:darkness names White and Black apart. Six are built: Holy Light (enemy pawns cannot take this king, and it cannot take pawns), Mercy (the king steps 1–2 and jumps friends, but takes only a guard), Death Touch (the king takes an adjacent enemy without moving), Darkness (pawns step diagonally and take straight ahead, no double step), March (pawns step two squares from any rank) and Leap (rooks, bishops and the queen pass over their own pawns).
